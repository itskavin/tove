Sundar Pichai is an Indian-born technology executive and the CEO of Google and its parent company, Alphabet.

[![Sundar Pichai](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c3/Sundar_Pichai_-_2023_%28cropped%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sundar_Pichai)

- **Early life:** He grew up in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and studied engineering at IIT Kharagpur.
- **At Google:** He helped lead products including Google Chrome and ChromeOS before becoming Google CEO in 2015.
- **Alphabet:** He became CEO of Google’s parent company in 2019.
- **Education:** He also studied at Stanford University and the Wharton School.
