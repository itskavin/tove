Jal Mahal is a lakeside photo stop, not a normal palace visit; don’t go down the stepwell stairs if access is restricted.[^3] Sources disagree on Amer’s opening time, so confirm it on the official booking portal before setting off.[^1][^2][^3]

## Budget for two

A reasonable planning allowance is **₹16,000–₹29,000 total**, excluding travel to Jaipur and shopping. These are estimates, not live quotes, and assume one shared room for two nights:

| Expense | Estimate for two |
| --- | ---: |
| Mid-range hotel, 2 nights | ₹6,000–₹12,000 |
| Meals and snacks, 3 days | ₹4,000–₹6,000 |
| Local autos and cabs | ₹3,000–₹5,000 |
| Monument tickets | ₹2,000–₹3,000 |
| Buffer | ₹1,000–₹3,000 |

The ticket estimate assumes Indian adult rates and standard entries; foreign visitor rates and premium palace tours cost more.[^2][^3] For a convenient base, look around **MI Road or C-Scheme**. A cab for the Amer–Nahargarh day makes sense; use autos or app cabs for shorter trips.

December mornings and evenings can be chilly, so pack a light jacket and comfortable shoes.[^2] Book the hotel early, especially if you’re travelling near Christmas or New Year.
